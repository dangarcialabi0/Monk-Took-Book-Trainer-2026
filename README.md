## 🎮 About the Game

Monk Took Book is a co-op action memory and library-management game developed and published by SquarePlay Games. It released on September 3, 2026, for Windows PC. Players organize hundreds of symbol-marked books in a medieval monastic library, remember their locations, fulfill nighttime requests, upgrade the monastery, and defend the collection from hostile creatures.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Monk Took Book**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and gameplay controls for managing the library, moving through the monastery, developing the monk, and surviving nighttime attacks. It is designed around the game's book organization, blessing upgrades, physical book handling, fire hazards, and combat systems.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want a relaxed solo experience, need help managing large book collections, want to explore the monastery without time pressure, or prefer to focus on library organization rather than combat and fire hazards.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Enter a playable session before enabling movement options. Increased Movement Speed helps with long trips between shelves, chambers, and delivery points. Use moderate values for more natural control, especially when carrying stacks of books or playing in third person.

### Tools & Inventory

Books are physical objects identified by symbols rather than titles, and players decide where to shelve them. Use Easy Book Handling while sorting, stacking, carrying, or throwing books. No Book Damage is useful when transporting valuable volumes through dangerous areas, but normal organization and delivery objectives still need to be completed.

### World & Environment

The library operates on a day-and-night cycle. New books arrive during the day, while nighttime brings requests from the Scribes and attacks on the collection. Freeze Night Timer provides extra time for deliveries and defense. Unlimited Blessings can be used for monk upgrades or for expanding the collection with additional shelves and chambers.

### Combat & Weapons

Monk Took Book includes melee and ranged defense with a dagger, crossbow, and holy cross. Activate Unlimited Health before entering dangerous areas, and use Unlimited Crossbow Bolts when relying on ranged attacks. No Fire Damage and No Book Damage help protect the monk and the collection from environmental hazards during attacks.
